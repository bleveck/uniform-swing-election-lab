II.  Replication Instructions for data in Figures using JOPrepfile1

Figure 1.  

a.  Gelman-King incumbency advantage:

. sort year
. by year:  reg dv dvp ptynow inc3 if redist==0&year!=1972& year!=1982& year!1992&year!=2002&year!=2012

Coefficient on “inc3” is the incumbency advantage

b.  Modified Gelman-King incumbency advantage:

. sort year
. by year:  reg dv dpres ptynow inc3

Coefficient on “inc3” is the incumbency  advantage
	
c.  Slurge (average of “sophomore surge” and “retirement slump”)

Sophomore surge:  difference in the change in vote share from the previous election for the party’s candidate (dv-dvp) in seats held by freshmen incumbents (fr=1) compared to other incumbents (fr=0), computed for each party for each year (opposite sign for Repubicans).  Includes only candidates with major party opposition in both current and previous election; freshmen includes new members elected in the previous election (fr=1), but not freshmen elected between the regular elections (fr=2, fr=3).  Calculate the average for both parties together.

Retirement slump:  difference in the change in vote share from the previous election between contests involving incumbents (inc=0 if Republican, inc=1 if  Democrat) compared to contests in districts held by the party with no incumbent running (inc=3 if Republican, inc=2 if Democrat), computed for each party for each year.  Includes only candidates with major party opposition in both current and previous election.  Calculate the average for both parties together.

Average the sophomore surge and retirement slumps to get the Slurge.
Figure 6.  

. by year: correl  dv dpres if midterm==0
. by year: correl  dv dpres if midterm==0&inc<2

Square the result, multiply by 100 to get percent of variance explained. 

Figure 7.

. tabulate year if midterm==0&split==1&inc<2
. tabulate year if midterm==0&split==1&inc>1

Take the frequencies for split results for each year in the series, divide by 4.35 to get the percentage of split results for each category (incumbents and non-incumbents).  

Figure 8.

. tabulate year if incwin==1&cprescat==2&inc==1&year!=1962 &year!=1966
. tabulate year if incwin==1&cprescat==2&inc==0&year!=1962 &year!=1966

Frequencies listed for each year in the results for incumbents of each party comprise the data points in this file.

Figure 9

. tabulate decade if cprescat==0, sum (incwin)
. tabulate decade if cprescat==2, sum (incwin)

Enter results as percentages for “leans to incumbent’s party,” “balanced,” and “leans to challengers party.”

Figure A1

Gelman-King:

. sort decade
. by decade: areg dv dvp ptynow inc3 if south==1&redist==0, absorb (year)
. by decade: areg dv dvp ptynow inc3 if south==0&redist==0, absorb (year)

Modified Gelman-King:

. sort decade
. by decade: areg dv dpres ptynow inc3 if south==1, absorb (year)
. by decade: areg dv dpres ptynow inc3 if south==0, absorb (year)
